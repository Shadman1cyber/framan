## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a synthesis of timeless Persian architectural heritage and precision hospitality engineering. Crafted for luxury dining establishments, boutique tea salons, and high-end culinary concepts, it balances poetic architectural gravitas with the dense, low-latency utility required in live point-of-sale, floor plan orchestrations, and back-of-house culinary displays.

The aesthetic philosophy draws directly from Isfahan tilework, vaulted brick naves (*Taq*), and calligraphic manuscript margins—distilled into a contemporary, high-order digital environment. Rather than applying superficial decoration, Persian spatial geometry governs rhythm, micro-borders, and elevated status nodes:
- **Heritage Craft Meets Operational Speed:** Tactile parchment surfaces, illuminated gold accents, and lapis structural panels coexist with crisp data tables and high-contrast typography.
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Eliminates clinical enterprise grays in favor of warm alabaster, crushed lapis lazuli, saffron-tinted highlights, and terracotta accents.
- **Architectural Cadence:** Vaulted framing, soft 8-pointed structural markers, and micro-latticework linear dividers define space while preserving peak ergonomic clarity during high-volume service rushes.

## Colors

The palette establishes hierarchy through authentic mineral and botanical pigments, grounding high-throughput operations in warmth and cultural weight:

- **Primary (`#122B48` - Lapis Lazuli / Persian Night):** Anchor tone for structural framing, core navigation surfaces, high-priority operational buttons, and high-contrast display headlines.
- **Secondary (`#0E8994` - Firoozeh / Persian Turquoise):** Used for focal action points, verified reservations, table seat transitions, and secondary interactive iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B26` - Zafaran / Saffron Gold):** Reserved for VIP guest indicators, pending kitchen tickets, glowing active tab rings, and luxury status accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5` - Warm Parchment Alabaster):** The tactile foundational canvas, supplemented by `#F4EFEB` (Surface Container Low) and `#E3DDD4` (Geometric Border Wire).
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - *Terracotta Clay (`#C35B3F`):* Critical alerts, 86'd inventory items, urgent order delays.
  - *Fresh Pistachio (`#4E8752`):* Course served, settled bill, kitchen ticket fulfilled.
  - *Rosewater Mist (`#F7EBE8`):* Subtle warning fills and VIP reservation highlights.
  - *Walnut Slate (`#1A1E24`):* Dark-mode subsurfaces, kitchen display (KDS) panels, and high-contrast data bars.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an intentional dichotomy between poetic hospitality ritual and strict transactional efficiency:

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys cultural authority and sensory elegance. Applied exclusively to section titles, guest book intros, revenue summary hero metrics, and modal headers. Never used for dense data tables or real-time order streams.
- **Functional Interface & Data Engine (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its geometric clarity, tall x-height, and open apertures. It governs floor layout seat badges, numerical ticket counters, inventory tables, and high-frequency touch targets.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced on all pricing columns, modifier prices, and kitchen countdown timers to eliminate baseline jitter during rapid status recalculations.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid 12-column grid system anchored by steady margins inspired by traditional illuminated manuscript proportions:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Complex table management grids and POS order splits leverage asymmetric 4:8 or 3:9 layouts.
- **Tablet / Terminal (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Used primarily for floor management iPads and kitchen terminal consoles; lateral action sidebars collapse into a warm floating utility drawer.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`. Dense order tickets stack vertically with fixed bottom operational bars for waitstaff mobility.
- **Rhythm:** Layout distances follow an uncompromising 8-point baseline scale. Compact operational panels use `space-sm` for dense grouping, while editorial guest profiles use `space-xl` to promote clarity and luxurious restraint.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering and warm ambient shadows tinted with ground Lapis and Umber, rejecting stark digital drop shadows:

- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Warm Parchment (`#FBF9F5`) with no shadow. Flat micro-latticework hairline borders (`1px solid #E3DDD4`).
- **Elevated Interactive Surfaces (Level 1 - Cards, Table Nodes):** `#FFFFFF` with a warm diffused tint: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(18, 43, 72, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(217, 155, 38, 0.03)`.
- **Floating Operational Layers (Level 2 - Modals, Split Check Drawers, Popovers):** Translucent Parchment Glass (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), framed with a crisp inner highlight border: `1px solid rgba(227, 221, 212, 0.8)` and drop shadow `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 43, 72, 0.08)`.
- **Illuminated Active State:** Elements in focus or tables requiring immediate attention utilize a delicate saffron radiance: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #D99B26, 0 4px 14px rgba(217, 155, 38, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances clean ergonomic usability with Persian architectural silhouettes:

- **Base Curvature:** Standard components use `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`), with cards and container wrappers utilizing `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **The Vaulted Arch (*Taq* Header):** High-level overview cards, section breaks, and menu category heroes incorporate asymmetric radii—defining the top-left and top-right boundaries with a graceful `1.5rem` arch, while bottom edges remain structured at `0.5rem`.
- **Arabesque Chamfers & Stars:** Status chips and table numbers incorporate subtle 8-pointed geometric profile indicators or micro-clipped 45-degree corner accents on selection.
- **Dividers:** Horizontal and vertical section dividers feature a centered micro-diamond motif or hairline geometry punctuated by a `#D99B26` saffron midpoint dot.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Persian Lapis (`#122B48`) fill with crisp alabaster text, `rounded: 0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. On hover, shifts to an opulent deep lapis sheen with subtle saffron perimeter radiance (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #D99B26`).
- **Secondary / Action:** Firoozeh Turquoise (`#0E8994`) background with crisp white text for quick affirmative actions (e.g., "Fire Course", "Seat Party").
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1px solid #E3DDD4` border, `#122B48` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F4EFEB`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill-shaped with micro-arabesque border structure.
  - *VIP Table:* Saffron tint fill (`rgba(217, 155, 38, 0.12)`), `#D99B26` text, micro 8-pointed gold star glyph prefix.
  - *Available:* Pistachio fill (`rgba(78, 135, 82, 0.1)`), `#4E8752` text.
  - *Urgent/Delayed:* Terracotta fill (`rgba(195, 91, 63, 0.1)`), `#C35B3F` text.

### Cards & Panels
- **Table / Order Cards:** Warm white base, micro-border in `#E3DDD4`. Cards feature a vaulted top contour (`rounded-t-2xl rounded-b-md`) for category headers.
- **Kitchen Display System (KDS) Cards:** Dark slate background (`#1A1E24`) paired with high-contrast amber and turquoise indicators, maintaining legibility under harsh kitchen lighting.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Inputs:** Crisp Alabaster fill (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E3DDD4` border, `0.5rem` radius. Labels styled in `label-md` with tracking `0.04em`. Focus state transitions the border to `#0E8994` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 137, 148, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Controls:** Custom square-with-chamfer design. Checked state displays a solid Lapis (`#122B48`) field housing a geometric saffron check icon. Radios feature concentric circular fills with a saffron center node.

### Lists & Tabular Data
- **Floor & Reservation Lists:** Striped alternating rows utilizing pure alabaster and `#FBF9F5`. Row dividing borders feature a `1px` continuous latticework hairline with `0.5px` vertical tracking guides. Numeric values (bill total, seated duration) are rendered strictly in tabular sans-serif figures.
